## Who they are
Notion is an all-in-one productivity and collaboration platform that combines
notes, docs, wikis, project management, and databases into a single workspace.

## Flagship capabilities
- **Docs & notes** — rich-text pages, nested hierarchy, inline embeds.
- **Databases** — relational tables with views (board, gallery, timeline, calendar).
- **Wikis** — team knowledge bases with permissions and version history.
- **Projects** — task tracking with dependencies and sprints.
- **Notion AI** — AI assistant for writing, summarizing, and Q&A over your content.
- **Templates gallery** — thousands of community + official starter templates.
- **Integrations** — Slack, GitHub, Figma, Linear, Google Drive, and many more.
- **Mobile + desktop apps** — cross-platform sync.

## Who uses it
Individuals, students, startups, and enterprises — particularly teams that want
flexibility over opinionated structure. Used by companies like Pixar, Headspace,
Figma, Amazon, and Loom for internal docs, wikis, and project coordination.

## What sets it apart
- Block-based editor — every element (text, image, database) is a movable block.
- Highly customizable — databases can be reshaped without schema migrations.
- Generous free tier for personal use.
- Strong community of template creators and consultants.

## Common competitor names (for disambiguation)
Obsidian, Roam Research, Logseq, ClickUp, Coda, Airtable, Evernote, Confluence,
Craft, Anytype, Apple Notes, Google Docs.

## Known-false claims to flag if an AI says them
- Any claim about specific pricing tiers or dollar amounts (Notion's pricing
  changes; we avoid asserting exact numbers without verification).
- Any claim that Notion is "fully offline-first" — it's cloud-first with
  offline read access.
- Any claim that Notion is only for personal use, or only for teams — it serves
  both.
